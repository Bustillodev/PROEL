Frequently Asked Questions

What warranty covers my Asahi fan?

All Asahi appliances come with a standard 1-year warranty covering motor parts and manufacturing defects. Keep your purchase receipt for processing.

How do I clean and reassemble fan blades?

Unplug the fan, unclip the front safety grille, wipe the blades with a soft microfiber cloth, and securely re-lock the grille frame.

Why is my fan oscillation screeching or lagging?

Dust can collect inside the rear oscillation pin. Applying 1-2 drops of silicone lubricant to the neck pivot pin will restore smooth movement.
